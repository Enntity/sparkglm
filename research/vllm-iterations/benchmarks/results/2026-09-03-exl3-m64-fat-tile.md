# EXL3 64-row fat-expert tile acceptance

Date: 2026-09-03

## Verdict

Accept for the opinionated two-GB10 GLM-5.3 Flash appliance.

The 64-row direct-trellis kernel is bit-exact with the accepted 128-row
kernel, wins the complete isolated expert pipeline throughout the measured
production range, and improves both warm medium-C2 and large-C2 endpoint
results. The 128-row symbols remain available as an explicit rollback through
`EXL3_FAT_TILE_M=128`.

This is a prefill optimization. It does not claim an isolated decode gain;
better aggregate decode in the staggered matrix comes from shortening the
competing prefill work.

## Why this shape

The synchronized long-prefill trace contains 6,589 paired gate/up and 6,589
down/scatter fat-kernel launches per rank. Its M128 grid-Y distribution shows:

- 36.08% of launches use at most two row blocks;
- 53.15% use at most three;
- 73.44% use at most five;
- mean grid-Y is 5.56, or about 712 rows at M128 granularity.

The accepted kernel carried eight 16-row accumulator fragments per thread,
96 registers per thread in the profile, and 23,552 bytes of dynamic shared
memory. The M64 specialization carries four fragments and 17,408 bytes of
dynamic shared memory. It also halves tail granularity while retaining the
same K16/N128 arithmetic and output epilogues.

## Endpoint result

The endpoint uses TP2, EXL3 K4 MCG, DFlash2 K7/TP2, FP8 KV, a 7,168-token
batch budget, four sequence slots, work-conserving mixed scheduling, five
seconds of request stagger, and 128 forced output tokens. The control values
are the immediately preceding restored accepted M128 service measurements.

| Case | M128 prefill | M64 prefill | Gain | M128 agg decode | M64 agg decode | Gain | M128 wall | M64 wall | Reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Warm medium C2 | 1,332.65 | 1,379.47 | +3.51% | 29.05 | 29.23 | +0.61% | 29.60 s | 28.84 s | 2.57% |
| Large C2 | 1,313.91 | 1,384.74 | +5.39% | 11.22 | 11.69 | +4.18% | 54.44 s | 51.93 s | 4.60% |

The first cold medium-C2 request was neutral on prefill (1,223.77 M128 versus
1,223.72 M64), while aggregate decode improved 2.34% and wall fell 1.60%.
This is consistent with one-time shape/JIT effects dominating that sample.

All six measured candidate requests completed 128/128 tokens with
`finish_reason=length`, included their own request marker, included no peer
marker, and returned no API error. Accepted/rejected prediction counters were
zero in both builds for these forced outputs, so no speculative-acceptance
claim is made from this workload.
